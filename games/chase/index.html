How to play Billa Chuha Chase

Move & aim

Hold WASD or arrow keys to move and use the mouse to aim. On touch screens, drag the joystick.

Abilities

Hold Space / E for ability 1 and Q for ability 2, or use the on-screen buttons. You can steer while using an ability.
