Trap Escape

Single playerPlatformerPuzzleActionRatsCats

Be the clever Rat in a manual chase platformer. The Cat follows your recorded route after a head start, slightly faster. Six carried items and thirteen stage traps include new ceiling slams, sliding floors, elevator launches, floor pistons, pendulum bonks, shutters and reversing conveyors. Pass their far levers to spring the machinery on the Cat, then chain its cartoon reactions. Ride or jump moving floors as you escape. Fifty stages span five worlds, with a boss chase in each. Quick retries, original mascots, optional sound and locally saved stars, times and progress keep every escape replayable. This is single player with an AI Cat.

How to play Trap Escape

Run & jump

A/D or Left/Right moves. W, Up or Space jumps. Touch uses a horizontal joystick and a separate Jump button. Movement starts the chase; P or Escape pauses.

Set your traps

Collect up to three trap items. E/F or DROP places the selected trap behind you. Q or Tab while the game canvas is focused cycles items. Tap inventory to select, tap again to drop, or swipe to cycle. Run beyond the far levers to arm ceiling slams, pistons, pendulums, shutters and other stage traps for the Cat. Sliding floors and elevators carry you and leave real gaps; conveyors move you in the arrow direction.

Escape & chain

Reach the door or land three Cat stuns. Hit again within three seconds for a combo. Boss chases require four stuns and a 2× chain. All nineteen trap types work with stun and combo scoring. Firecrackers have a two-second fuse; magnets pull nearby armed metal traps, including machinery, into the combo.

Stars & progress

Three stars for a Cat knockout, a 3× chain or beating par. Two for escaping with any stun; one for an escape. Clear each boss and earn 15 stars in its world to unlock the next. Best times, stars and retries save on this browser; no account is needed.
