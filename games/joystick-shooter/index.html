Cat Chase

Single playerPlatformerActionPuzzleCatsRats

Control the Cat in an original platform chase inspired by surprise-trap games. Run, jump and pounce after an AI Rat through 50 stages across five worlds. Thirteen trap families include wide ceiling slams, shifting ground, elevator floors, rising pistons, swinging wrecking balls, timed shutters and conveyors, alongside crumbling floors, spikes, falling crates, sliding walls, springs and fake exits. Different early hazards and combinations make each world’s chases varied. Catch the Rat, collect bonus bells and retry quickly. Stars, best times and stage unlocks save on this browser. Keyboard and separate touch controls support manual play.

How to play Cat Chase

Move the Cat

A/D or Left/Right moves. W, Up or Space jumps. Touch uses a horizontal joystick and separate Jump and Pounce buttons. Movement starts the chase. P or Escape pauses.

Pounce & catch

E/F or Shift pounces in the direction you face, with a 1.35-second cooldown. Catch the Rat by touching it. Pounce can extend a jump but cannot pass through walls or protect against traps. The AI Rat pauses at bait and waits briefly at its exit.

Read the machinery

Wait outside striped ceiling-slam zones, cross shutters while open and let floor pistons retract. Ride sliding/elevator floors, time the swinging wrecking ball, and follow conveyor arrows. Cracked floors, spikes, crates, walls, springs and fake exits return in varied combinations. Warnings mark each danger; failures retry automatically.

50 stages & local progress

Catch the Rat to unlock the next stage. Three bonus bells, or beating par without a retry, earns three stars. Catch within two retries for two stars; every catch earns at least one. Replay cleared stages to improve stars and best times. This is single player with an AI Rat, and progress saves on this browser.
