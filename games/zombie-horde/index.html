Zombie Horde

MultiplayerActionSurvivalCo-opRatsCatsDogs

Team up online as Rat, Cat and Dog. Choose Endless Survival to explore five supply areas across a 2400 by 1800 map, or Capture the Zone to cross a sprawling warehouse, crew mounted guns, defend checkpoints and defeat the extraction boss. Both missions include jumping, rechargeable jetpacks and character abilities. Three players share each run, with bots filling empty slots.

How to play Zombie Horde

Move & fire

Move with WASD / arrows and aim with the mouse. Click or hold Space to fire. Touch uses a movement joystick, FIRE and nearby-enemy auto-aim.

Abilities & supplies

J jumps; hold Shift, V or JET to fly. Rat: E jump, Q decoy, C dash. Cat: E speed boost, Q hide, C pounce. Dog: E chain hook, Q bark. R reloads; F swaps weapons. Land to collect ammo, weapons and medkits. White first-aid stations heal nearby players.

Capture the Zone

Choose Capture the Zone in the game menu. Hold each marked ring for 20 seconds; zombies contest capture. T / USE TURRET crews a mounted gun; move or press T again to dismount. Secure four zones and defeat the final boss to extract.

Play together

Sign in with your existing Billa Chuha account. Use Quick Play or create a private room and share its invitation. One Rat, Cat and Dog; empty slots receive bots at start. Each mission has its own Quick Play queue.
