Dungeon Dash

Single playerRoguelikeActionPlatformerRatsCatsDogs

Jump & Fire has become Dungeon Dash: a solo roguelike platformer with fresh rooms, six enemy types, four weapons and endless floors. Clear enemies to unlock each exit, face a phased boss every tenth room and shop safely every five rooms. Keep unspent gems after death or retreat and buy seven permanent upgrades. Explore Kitchen, Garden, Rooftop, Sewer and Inferno themes with double jumps, auto-aim, combo rewards and optional player-reported deepest-room rankings. Progress saves locally on this device.

How to play Dungeon Dash

Move & jump

Use A/D or Left/Right to move, W/Up to jump twice, and S/Down to drop through platforms. Touch has a movement joystick and a separate Jump button. All movement and combat are manually controlled.

Fire & equip

Hold Space or FIRE to shoot with nearby-enemy auto-aim. F / SWAP or the weapon icon picks up a nearby weapon, otherwise swaps your two carried weapons. Pistol ammo is unlimited; SMG, shotgun and rocket ammo is limited. Rockets can hurt you up close.

Rooms & bosses

Defeat every enemy, then walk through the unlocked door on the right. Enemies and floors get harder as long as you survive. Every tenth room has a boss. Stomp enemies or shoot; attack shielded dogs from behind or above, and destroy the Dog General’s turret to break its shield.

Gems & upgrades

A safe shop every five rooms spends only your current-run gems. Unspent gems are banked when you die or retreat. Buy seven permanent upgrades between runs. P pauses; Retreat & keep gems safely ends the run. Progress stays on this browser, and online deepest-room scores are optional after signing in.
