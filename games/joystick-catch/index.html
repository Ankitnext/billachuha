Kitchen Heist

Single playerStealthPuzzleActionRatsCatsDogs

Sneak in. Grab the cheese. Get out alive. Control Rat through 50 top-down stealth stages across Home Kitchen, Restaurant, Factory, Warehouse and Secret Lab. Furniture blocks visible Cat vision cones; sprinting and metal floors make noise. Use moving cheese decoys, smoke bombs, one-use mouse tunnels and Soft Paws to outsmart patrols and hunting bosses. Dog cameras, conveyors, darkness and timed lasers change the route. Earn stars, improve best times and save progress locally. No account is needed.

How to play Kitchen Heist

Sneak & sprint

WASD or arrows move in eight directions. Hold Shift to sprint, or use the touch joystick and hold Sprint. Walking is quiet; sprinting and metal grates can attract nearby Cats. Choose keys and Customize HUD are available in the toolbar.

Steal & escape

Collect every cheese to unlock the exit. Yellow Cat cones and Dog spotlights stop at furniture and smoke. Cats pause in alert, chase after 1.5 seconds, then search after losing sight for three seconds. Camera beams and red lab lasers trigger a ten-second alarm. P / Escape pauses.

Stealth tools

Walk over glowing caches to carry up to three tools. E / Space or Use Tool activates the selected item; Q, Cycle or an inventory swipe cycles it. Decoy lures a patrol, Smoke blocks vision, Mouse Hole needs an entrance and exit 40–400px apart, and Soft Paws silences Rat for five seconds. Tap inventory slots to select.

50 jobs & hunter stages

Every tenth job has a hunting Cat. Collect its two double-charge tools, distract the nearby hunter four times, take all cheese and escape. Three stars require no sightings and beating par; two require at most one sighting. Clear a boss and earn 15 stars to open the next world. Saves and best times remain in this browser; no online rankings are submitted.
