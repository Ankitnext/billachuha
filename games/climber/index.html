Sky Siege

SoloPlatformerTower DefenseStrategyRatsCatsDogs

A vertical platformer fused with tower defense. Jump upward as Rat through seeded shelves, collect cheese and leave Butter Traps, Magnet Mines, Firecrackers and friendly Dog Guards on passed platforms. Waves of Walkers, Jumpers, armored Tanks and hidden Ninjas climb after you every five layers. Kitchen, Garden, Rooftop, Sewer and Inferno themes mark your ascent. Earn milestone badges, bank unspent earned cheese and buy seven permanent upgrades between runs. Scores and upgrades save locally; no account is needed.

How to play Sky Siege

Move & jump

A/D or Left/Right moves. W, Up or Space jumps; release and tap again for a double jump. Touch has a joystick and a separate Jump button. Hold toward a world edge while falling to slide slowly. Choose keys and Customize HUD are available in the toolbar.

Build below

Q / Cycle or a swipe changes defense types. Tap a trap to select it. E / Place builds the selected defense on the shelf Rat stands on, spending cheese. Each shelf holds one defense; airborne placement is blocked. Butter costs 5, Magnet 10, Firecracker 15 and Dog Guard 25 before discounts.

Hold off the Cats

Every five layers brings a two-second wave warning. Walkers climb one shelf, Jumpers skip a layer, Tanks need two hits and Ninjas hide until nearby. Defend landing shelves, use blast/reveal tools and keep climbing. A Dog Guard lasts 20 seconds or three punches. Falling below the camera or Cat contact ends the run.

Score & upgrades

Score equals total earned cheese times highest layer reached. Unspent earned cheese banks on death, retreat or interrupted-run recovery. Buy seven permanent upgrades between runs. Packed-lunch starter cheese funds traps and cannot be banked. Milestone rewards are once per profile. Best scores and upgrades stay in this browser.
