Arena Blitz

MultiplayerActionPlatformerTeam battleRatsCatsDogs

Join Alpha or Bravo as Rat, Cat or Dog in a six-player online arena. Explore a spacious 2400 by 1100 arena with wide platforms and spread-out team spawns. A following camera keeps players large, while the minimap shows both teams and weapon locations. Use a fuel-limited jetpack, collect weapons, throw grenades and race to 20 kills. Five-minute rounds, respawns and sudden death keep the battle moving. Bots fill empty slots.

How to play Arena Blitz

Move & fly

A / D or arrows move. Space jumps; hold W / Up / Space to use the jetpack. S / Down drops through platforms. Touch uses the movement joystick and JET / JUMP. Your camera follows you; the arena minimap shows the whole stage, both teams and weapons.

Fight & collect

Aim with the mouse and click to fire; touch uses nearby-enemy auto-aim. Q / right click throws a grenade, E melees, R reloads and F / PICK UP swaps weapons.

Win as a squad

Each team has one Rat, Cat and Dog. First to 20 kills wins, or highest score after five minutes. Ties enter sudden death without respawns. Create rooms for friends or use Quick Play.
