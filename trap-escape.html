Collect up to three trap items. Drop them behind you. Run past marked ropes and levers to arm the stage traps safely. The Cat follows you into them. Hit it again within three seconds to build a chain; a slipping Cat can slide into another trap.

New machinery: Run past the far lever to arm ceiling slams, floor pistons, pendulums, shutters and reversing conveyors for the Cat. Sliding floors and elevators physically carry you and can leave a gap: ride them or jump across. Machinery hits count toward Cat stuns and combos. All six carried items and the original stage traps are still available.

Inventory: tap an item to select it, tap it again to drop it, or swipe the slots to cycle. Firecrackers have a two-second fuse and a blast radius. Magnets pull nearby armed metal traps into a chain.
