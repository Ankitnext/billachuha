Know who's climbing.

Score = total earned cheese × highest layer. Spending cheese on traps reduces your take-home wallet, while all cheese earned still counts toward score. Cat defeats, complete wave clears and every 25 layers grant more cheese. Outrunning a wave doesn't earn its clear bonus.

Seven permanent upgrades improve jumps, speed, discounts, blast radius, Dog duration, pickup range and starting supplies. Unspent earned cheese banks on death or retreat. Packed-lunch starter cheese funds defenses and cannot be repeatedly banked. Profile milestones grant one-time badges and wallet rewards.

Hold toward a world edge while falling to slide slowly. Falling below the camera ends the run. Pausing, hiding this tab and opening control settings stop the action. No account is needed.
