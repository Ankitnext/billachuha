FIELD NOTES

Every square has a story.

Roll, then play. Each roll makes a short platform stage. Clear its finish flag to move. Miss it and stay on your starting cell; keep your coins. Reach 100 to win. You don’t need an exact roll.

Ladders: climb moving stairs to reach the top. A fall or timeout keeps you at the foot. Snakes: steer around thorns while falling. Each coin saves one cell of the drop, up to one cell below the snake’s head.

Power cells: a shield, extra jump or speed lasts for your next three turns. Guardians: stomp the boss from above, or shoot as Dog. Win four bonus cells; lose your next turn. A skipped solo turn costs eight seconds.

Rat gets +1 cell per roll, two jumps and a wall slide. Cat avoids its first snake and has a speed boost. Dog can reroll once per game before playing a move and shoots enemies.

Keyboard: A/D or arrows move; W/Up/Space jumps. E activates Cat speed or Dog fire; F also fires as Dog. P pauses. Touch uses the joystick and separate action buttons. Solo scores reward progress, coins, defeated enemies and time remaining.

Pass & play is local: take turns on the same device. Solo wins can be saved to the shared leaderboard after signing in. Community scores are reported by players.
