A FIELD GUIDE FOR SMALL ADVENTURERS

Clear. Collect. Go deeper.

Defeat every enemy to unlock the door on the right. Walk through it to enter a new room. Rooms keep getting harder for as long as you survive. Every fifth room leads to a safe shop; every tenth room has a boss.

Move: A/D or Left/Right. Jump: W/Up, twice in the air. S/Down drops through a platform. Fire: hold Space. Shots aim at the nearest enemy. Pick up / swap: F or the weapon button. A nearby weapon takes priority. P pauses.

Touch has a movement joystick and separate Jump, Fire and Swap buttons. You can hold Fire while moving and jumping. The pistol has unlimited bullets; other weapons return to the pistol when empty. Rockets can hurt you if they explode nearby.

Stomp enemies from above. Shoot shielded enemies from behind or from above. The Dog General’s final shield is powered by its turret: destroy the turret first. When one Twin Cat falls, the other enrages.

Kill enemies within three seconds for a gem-multiplying chain. The safe shop spends this run’s gems. The gems left when you die or retreat go to your permanent wallet. Buy upgrades between runs. Saved progress is local to this browser; clearing its data removes it.

Sound and motion effects can be switched off. Online rankings are optional, require your existing account and use player-reported deepest-room scores.
